Visi & Misi

Vision & Mission

Visi

Vision

Terwujudnya kaum difabel yang terampil, mandiri dan bermartabat

Realization of skilled, independent and dignified people with disabilities

Misi

Mission

  1. 1. Mengadvokasi kepentingan kaum difabel agar martabatnya sebagai manusia yang mulia terwujud
  2. 1. Advocating for the interests of people with disabilities so that their dignity as noble humans is realized
  3. 2. Pendampingan kaum difabel dari segi kesehatan, ketrampilan dan pengetahuan yang memadai
  4. 2. Assisting people with disabilities in terms of health, skills and adequate knowledge
  5. 3. Kaum difabel memperoleh kesejahteraan sesuai yang diharapkan
  6. 3. People with disabilities achieve the expected welfare
